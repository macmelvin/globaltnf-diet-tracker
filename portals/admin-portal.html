Un-bind a gym login

Strips the hidden gym tag from an account — use it if a gym was deleted while its owner still carried the tag. The account keeps its data and (if it's yours) its admin access.

Testing · reset to clean slate

Permanently deletes all gyms, gym-owner logins, member enrollments, pending invites and billing snapshots so you can run a clean test. Member app accounts and their tracker data are not touched. This cannot be undone.

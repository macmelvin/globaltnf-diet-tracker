🏋️ Built with gyms in mind

Retention doesn't happen at the gym. It happens on the days they don't come in.

A gym visit happens twice a week. Diet Tracker happens every day — giving members a reason to stay engaged on the days they'd otherwise forget you exist, and giving your coaches real numbers instead of a shrug.

  • 📅 Daily check-ins between visits, not just twice-a-week gym time
  • 💬 Real data for your coaches to start real conversations
  • 🎯 A habit that keeps your gym top-of-mind on the days members would otherwise drift
